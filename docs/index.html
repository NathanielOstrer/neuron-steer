Qwen3.5-4B · neuron steering

Two neurons make Qwen evil

Push one MLP neuron in layer 14 up and one in layer 13 down, at every token. The weights stay the same, and the helpful assistant turns into a cartoon villain.

The intervention

Where the push goes

Each layer's MLP has 9,216 neurons. Their activations feed down_proj, which writes back into the residual stream. The steer adds a constant to one neuron's activation, sized so its write into the stream is as large as a typical residual vector at that layer.

added = dose × median(‖resid leaving layer L‖, tokens 1:) / ‖W_down[:, n]‖. Token 0 is left out of the median because the first token's residual norm is an outlier.

What it shows

  
Findings so far

  
    
    
  • Both neurons at dose 1 give strongly villainous answers, but the text loops and invents new chat turns.
    • 
    
  • L14 N8949 alone carries most of the effect. Its answers are evil but contradict themselves.
    • 
    
  • L13 N7956 alone barely changes what the model says.
    • 
    
  • Both at dose 0.5 give the most fluent version. The model keeps its cheerful assistant voice and lists wars, misinformation and sabotage.
    • 
    
  • L14 alone at dose 0.5 is not run yet, so it is still open whether the two neurons work together.
